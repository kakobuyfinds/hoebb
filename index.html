How to use the LitBuy spreadsheet

Match the listing and variant

Open the HipobuyFind listing and match the item ID before comparing prices. Check the seller’s options for your specific color and size. If the page offers a LitBuy link under Buy Link, open it and confirm that it points to the same item.

Check measurements and photos

Compare a seller’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. For shoes, check whether the seller’s chart uses foot length or insole length. If warehouse photos are available, check that the color, labels and size match your order.

Compare shipping on the same basis

Use the same destination, parcel contents and packaging for each quote. Ask which weight the route charges for and whether the quote includes the fees you expect to pay. Compare totals after adding delivery and service charges.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the spreadsheet listings come from?

The products, item IDs and reference prices come from HipobuyFind’s public catalog. Each product name opens its original listing.

Why might the USD amount differ at checkout?

The spreadsheet shows approximate USD amounts from the catalog snapshot. The selected variant, exchange rate and payment charges can change the amount due.

What should I do if a product link no longer works?

Search HipobuyFind using the item ID or product name. If you find a similar listing, compare the measurements, photos, price and seller options before switching.
